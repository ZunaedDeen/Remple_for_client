There’s something about fall that makes you want to get your house in order.

The temperatures cool down, the leaves start changing, Thanksgiving is around the corner, and suddenly all those little projects you’ve been saying “I’ll deal with that later” about start looking a lot more urgent.

And if your garage, basement, backyard or storage area has accumulated a little more junk than you’d like to admit, fall might actually be the perfect time to tackle it.

Here are five reasons why.

## 1. The Weather Makes It Easier

Let’s be honest: cleaning out a garage or hauling junk around the yard in the middle of a Saskatchewan summer is not exactly anyone’s idea of fun.

Fall brings cooler, more comfortable temperatures that make big outdoor jobs much more manageable. You can spend a few hours sorting, lifting, hauling and organizing without feeling like you're melting in the process.

It’s also a lot more motivating to tackle those bigger jobs when you’re not battling extreme heat.

Before the snow arrives, take advantage of those crisp fall days and get the heavy lifting done.

## 2. A Cleaner Yard Means Better Curb Appeal

Your yard is one of the first things people see when they come to your home—and clutter can make even a well-maintained property look messy.

Old furniture, broken equipment, leftover renovation materials, piles of wood and miscellaneous items that have accumulated throughout the summer can quickly become an eyesore.

!img d0f614_e44cdf4d6216483aa5d6d0700fca31b3~mv2.jpg

A fall cleanout gives you the opportunity to clear away the things you no longer need and leave your property looking clean and cared for before winter sets in.

And if you're thinking about selling your home, decluttering can make an even bigger difference. A clean, tidy exterior helps potential buyers see the property rather than everything you've accumulated around it.

## 3. Decluttering Can Help Clear Your Mind, Too

Junk isn't just taking up physical space. Sometimes, it's taking up mental space.

That pile in the garage you've been meaning to deal with? The broken furniture sitting in the basement? The boxes you haven't opened in three years?

Every time you see them, your brain gets another little reminder: I still need to deal with that.

Decluttering can help create a calmer, more organized environment and can reduce some of the stress associated with feeling like there is always another unfinished task waiting for you.

Getting those lingering projects off your mental to-do list before winter can also give you a greater sense of accomplishment and make your home feel more relaxing.

Sometimes, getting rid of the junk is about more than making room. It's about making room in your head, too.

## 4. Get Your Home Ready for Winter and the Holidays

Fall doesn't last forever.

Before long, we'll be spending more time indoors, pulling out the holiday decorations, hosting family and friends, and looking for places to store everything that comes with the winter season.

Thanksgiving is just around the corner, followed by Halloween, Christmas and the rest of the holiday season.

So why not make room now?

Getting rid of things you no longer use means you won't have to spend the winter storing, moving and working around stuff that serves no purpose.

Clear out the garage before you need to park the vehicle inside. Make room in the basement for holiday decorations. Get rid of old furniture before guests arrive.

The goal isn't just to have a clean house. It's to make your home work better for you.

## 5. Don't Give Pests a Place to Move In

As temperatures start to drop, rodents and other pests begin looking for warm, sheltered places to spend the winter.

And unfortunately, a pile of junk in your yard can make a pretty appealing home.

Old boxes, furniture, wood, appliances and other clutter can create sheltered spaces where rodents and other unwanted pests can hide and nest. Once they find a comfortable spot, you may have a much bigger problem on your hands.

Clearing away unnecessary debris around your property before winter can help eliminate some of those potential hiding places.

Because the last thing anyone wants to discover in the spring is that their backyard junk pile became someone's winter residence.

## Make Fall Your Fresh Start

You don't have to wait for spring cleaning.

Fall is the perfect time to look around your home and ask yourself a simple question:

> “What am I going to wish I had dealt with before the snow comes?”

Maybe it's the garage you've been avoiding. Maybe it's the old furniture in the basement. Maybe it's a pile of renovation leftovers sitting in the backyard.

Whatever it is, getting it done now means one less thing hanging over your head when winter arrives.
