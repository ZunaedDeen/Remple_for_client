Fall has a way of making us notice all the stuff we’ve been ignoring.

That patio chair that’s been sitting broken since July. The BBQ that hasn’t worked in two years. The pile of renovation debris beside the garage that you keep telling yourself you’ll deal with “next weekend.”

Well, winter is coming—and if you don’t want to look at that junk all winter, now is the perfect time to LET IT GO.

Here are six things worth getting rid of before the snow arrives.

## 1. Old Patio Furniture

!img d0f614_e0aa16eb139048c8a3d8cd5059f91445~mv2.jpg

Summer is over, and if that patio furniture has seen better days, there’s no reason to drag it through another Saskatchewan winter.

Broken chairs, cracked tables, faded cushions and old outdoor furniture can take up valuable space and become an even bigger mess once the snow hits.

If you’re not going to use it next summer, let it go now.

## 2. That Broken BBQ

!img d0f614_778565362e404e199af62417e7006cd2~mv2.jpg

You had good intentions. Maybe you were going to fix it. Maybe you were going to replace a part.

But if your BBQ has been sitting unused all summer, chances are it isn’t suddenly going to become a priority in January.

Get rid of the old one and make room for something you’ll actually use.

## 3. Old Yard Equipment

!img d0f614_9f2c3213db1c4a0b97fa12582cf0d780~mv2.jpg

Fall is the perfect time to take a look at your shed and garage.

Broken lawn mowers. Old trimmers. Rusty tools. Empty planters. Equipment you upgraded years ago but never got around to getting rid of.

You don’t need to store things you no longer use for another six months.

## 4. Renovation Debris

!img d0f614_65875a746b7e48cbb617b6e80ff4715c~mv2.jpg

Finished that renovation project months ago but still have leftover materials, scraps and debris sitting around?

Don’t let your renovation leftovers become part of the winter landscape.

Whether it’s old flooring, broken cabinets, construction materials or other renovation debris, getting it gone now means one less thing to deal with when spring arrives.

## 5. Old Appliances

!img d0f614_351409b99ece4cfab8bb625f9447d010~mv2.jpg

That old fridge in the garage. The broken washer in the basement. The appliance you replaced but never actually got rid of.

Big appliances are difficult to move and even harder to ignore.

If it’s broken, outdated or simply taking up space, fall is a great time to clear it out.

## 6. The Mysterious Pile Beside the Garage

!img d0f614_bbd772caef314c6996ce97a30acb0b05~mv2.jpg

You know the one.

It’s been there for months.

Nobody is quite sure what half of it is, and somehow it keeps getting bigger.

Every household has one.

Fall is your chance to finally deal with it.

## Don’t Let Your Junk Hibernate

Winter already brings enough things to worry about. Your junk pile doesn’t need to spend the season sitting outside, collecting snow and becoming an even bigger spring project.

A little fall cleanup can make your property look better, free up space and give you one less thing to deal with when the snow melts.
